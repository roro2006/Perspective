# Teaser (60 seconds)

A mood piece, not an explainer. No robots, no old product, no rebrand. The
idea runs both ways: an AI agent that sees from your perspective **learns
from you**, and because it sees what you see, **you learn from it**.

The voiceover is about 140 words, read slowly. It needs captions, because it
will often play muted.

## The idea, in the order the video shows it
1. **Attention:** what you look at is a free signal of what matters.
2. **AI learns from you:** your view tells it what you mean, and your
   reactions are feedback you never had to type. That human-side data barely
   exists today.
3. **You learn from AI:** an agent that sees from your perspective can teach
   in context. It notices where you're stuck, points at what you missed, and
   explains what's in front of you instead of something generic.
4. **The loop:** each side makes the other better.

The "you learn" beat quietly carries over our tutoring and classroom work
without naming the old product.

## Production notes
- The core footage is first-person (head-cam or glasses) shots of varied
  everyday work and learning: a desk, a textbook, a screen, hands-on work. The
  variety keeps it about "AI agents," not one domain.
- The highlights, pulses, model visual and flipping arrow are simple motion
  graphics (DaVinci Resolve Fusion or After Effects). Keep them minimal and
  warm.
- Avoid robot arms, anything that reads as robotics, and anything that looks
  like the old screen companion.
