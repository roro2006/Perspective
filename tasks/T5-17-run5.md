## Goal
Run 4 (0.50 ring-on-target on 100 scenes) mostly misses by closing 2-3 cm high with a jaw
resting on the block, 1-2 cm off along the closing axis, and it rarely rises and retries.
Run 4's noise faded out over the last 1.5-4 cm, so the data holds few such states. Run 5:

- **Noise reaches the block**: no fade (or a much shorter one), so the noisy descent
  sometimes ends with a jaw on the block or 1-2 cm off.
- **The expert recovers from exactly those states**: at the bottom it checks the jaw/block
  geometry (expert.straddles, expert.grasp_error); on a jaw on the block or a misalignment it
  rises a little, re-centres on where the block is now, comes down and closes only while
  straddling. Labels are the expert's corrective commands.
- Guard against run 4's early closes (frames 5-10) if the data explains them.

Keep demo episodes within the 60-frame (6 s) evaluation horizon. Record about 800 episodes
(varied wording, wrist camera, seeds 7000+), train with run-4 settings (bf16, chunk 30, 10
actions per call, about 3 h at most) into out/smolvla/run5.

## Gate (fixed before training)
On the T5-08 evaluation with n = 100 scenes (seed 50000, default settings):
- ring on target: target picks >= 0.60;
- ring on the partner: partner picks > target picks;
- words only: target picks < ring-on-target target picks.
Diagnosis (scripts/smolvla_diag.py, 30 scenes) and end to end (words, lora2, oracle;
out/e2e/run5, trials and choices copied from out/e2e/run4) are reported, not gated.
