  out/t5_23/run1_broken_render_*. Fix: a blank or frozen head frame, a blank robot frame, or

- **Perception**: colour segmentation (hue/saturation) plus rendered depth, back-projected with
  the head pose, clustered in 1 cm voxels; block centre from the visible top face. I didn't
  use the project's SegmentationDetector because it reads ground-truth segmentation. The
  hand is found by skin hue more than 3 cm above the table. Head pose carries tracking noise
  (3 mm, 0.5 deg per frame). Tracking: a block at rest has a 3 cm gate. A step starts when
  the block is lifted with the hand at it and ends when the hand has stayed away for 3 frames
  while the block rests on something; the rest position is the median of every sighting.
- **Plan and retargeting**: steps are on X, left/right of X (the person's), my left/right
  zone, or a fallback pose in the person's frame. Ids are colour_k from the person's left.
  On the new layout the robot fuses its camera (blocks it sees whole) with the mean of 8 head
  frames: the parked arm hides the near table from the robot camera. It names blocks the same
  way and turns each step into a goal point. The baseline reads the same words in the robot's
  frame. The expert pick grasps from the true block pose (as in T5-04). Perceived blocks are
  matched to the nearest sim block.
- **place()** (sim/place.py): carries at a clearance height on an IK line with fixed jaw yaw,
  measures the block's offset in the gripper frame, goes down, opens to 0.7 rad and rises
  slowly. A 0.45 opening and fast rise dragged stacked blocks off. Near the base the rise
  leans outwards.
- **Decisions the spec left open / worries**: (1) Demos are scripted to be clear: placements
  squarely 6 cm beside, no other block within 10 cm sideways, travel of 5 cm or more, ids
  stable (same-colour blocks 4 cm apart sideways). One demo can't separate intent in general.
  (2) New layouts are kept only if both Curio and the baseline can place every step, so the
  baseline fails by frame, not reach. (3) The baseline keeps person-frame ids, so only the
  relation reading differs. (4) Tuning used dev seeds 1000-1124. Gate seeds 0-4 were looked at
  once early on, with very different code. (5) The demo clip was descoped by the user, so
  there's no video. (6) Rendering needs the GPU's GL context, even though everything else is
  CPU.
