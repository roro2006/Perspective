# T5-05 notes

- `curio_robot/sim/demos.py`: `record(n_episodes, seed, out_dir, width, height, include_failed=False,
  overwrite=False) -> summary`, `load_info`, `load_episode(out_dir, i, images=False)`, `words_for`.
  Layout as collab/export.py: `meta/info.json`, `meta/episodes.jsonl`, `data/episode_*.jsonl`,
  `images/episode_*/{robot,human}_{frame:04d}.png`. CLI: `curio-sim demos --n --seed --out`.
- Scenes: attempt a = `fetch_scene(default_rng(seed + a))` with no box, target block
  `rng.integers(0, 5)` from the same generator. No occluder on purpose: the expert picks a hidden
  block only ~18/40 (T5-04 notes), so "hidden" demos wait for a better expert.
- Frames at 10 Hz (every 5th 20 ms tick) plus the start frame. Frame f's action is the ctrl in
  force when frame f+1 is observed (the expert's ramp sampled at 10 Hz); the last frame holds.
- target_pixel is the target block's current centre projected into the robot camera (it follows
  the block once lifted); None outside the image. It is a projection, not a visibility check: the
  arm covers the block during the grasp. episodes.jsonl also stores the start pixel.
- Words: unique colour "Hand me the blue one"; pair block "Hand me the red one on my left/right",
  side by `perspective.pick` with the person's camera pose (frame "speaker"). No "your" frame yet.
- Failed picks: skipped by default (the next seed takes their place, up to 5 tries per episode);
  `include_failed=True` keeps them with "success": false and their reason.
- 20 episodes at 320x240: 20/20 success, 840 frames, 1680 PNGs, 15.7 s, 70 MB on disk.
