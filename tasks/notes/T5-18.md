# T5-18 notes: PoC video

- Built scripts/poc/: one poseable capsule humanoid (kit.py), primitive robots (robots.py),
  six scenes plus the face-to-face shot (scenes_a.py, scenes_b.py), grounding glue
  (scene_base.py), PIL overlays (compose.py), the driver (make_video.py), and a cv2 encoder
  that runs in the lerobot-rocm venv because the project env has no cv2 (encode_cv2.py).
  Nothing was downloaded.
- The grounding is real. Each scene renders the head camera, builds an ObjectMap in the
  speaker's head frame, and goes through place_marks, render_marks and build_request (the
  geometry condition) to Qwen3-VL-4B Q8_0 with the r16 v2 adapter on llama-server :8081.
  Every query is logged in out/poc/grounding.json. The robot drives to the grounder's argmax.
- Results in the speaker's frame: dog, drone, home, ROV, space and warehouse order 2 were
  correct (p 1.00). The warehouse line "the rack behind me" was WRONG: the grounder picked the
  tote in front of the speaker (p 0.65). The video shows the reroute reaching the wrong rack
  with a red X, and the end grid marks the warehouse wrong. The robot-frame reading picked the
  wrong object in all six scenes.
- A decision the spec left open: the shipped prompt names only left and right, and an object
  behind the speaker can't be in their camera. So "behind me" uses depth_request, which is
  build_request's layout plus "in front of / behind" for each viewer, with letters for
  objects out of view. The adapter never saw that wording, and it is the one miss.
- I fixed two scene bugs before the final run: depth_request wrote "behind of the", and the
  astronaut's left wrench was outside the head camera (fixed with neck pitch). The first-pass
  answers are in out/poc/grounding_firstpass.json. The warehouse was wrong there too. Space
  was wrong there only because the target was out of view.
- Latencies were 26-135 ms. Repeated images hit llama-server's prompt cache, so these numbers
  are optimistic.
- Weak spots: in the drone shot the perspective makes the drone look close to the person; a
  green rack light clips a corner of the space shot; the coral looks like mushrooms; there is
  no contact physics (everything is kinematic); the robots don't pick anything up.
