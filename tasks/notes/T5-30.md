# T5-30 notes: why the trained pi0 did nothing at test time

- **Root cause:** a host-to-GPU copy race in T5-28's training loader. Batches used
  pinned memory, and LeRobot copies them to the RX 7900 XT with non_blocking=True. While
  the GPU was still busy with the previous step, the normalizer often saw an action
  chunk that wasn't the batch's own. In an exact rerun, 5 of 8 checked steps scored a
  different loss from the same batch re-read after a synchronise, with actions off by
  up to 9 normalized units. So pi0 learned corrupted targets: the logged training loss
  was about 0.001, but the weights score about 1 on clean batches and miss the demos by
  0.47 rad open loop.
- **Fix** (c3a0b8c): `pi0_data.loader_options` keeps pin_memory off, and pi0_train uses
  it. The unpinned loader corrupted 0 of 60 steps. `scripts/pi0_check.py` reproduces the
  losses, the open-loop error and the copy-race check. tests/test_t5_30_pi0_fix.py is
  the regression test.
- **Retrain:** the 022000 checkpoint is unusable, so the card's one retrain started
  (T5-28 recipe, out/t5_30/pi0). Held-out loss was 0.106 at step 2k, 0.093 at 4k, 0.084
  at 6k and 0.076 at 8k, against T5-28's 1.18, 0.71 and about 1.0. The run was **stopped
  by the user at step about 8,900 (161 min)**. Checkpoints 006000 and 008000 are kept.
- **Gate:** not run. Precondition P and the rollouts on seeds 53000-53099 haven't been
  attempted, and no eval-seed score exists. The card is blocked, not failed.
- **Open:** scripts/smolvla_train.py also uses pin_memory=True, so SmolVLA runs 2-5 may
  have trained on partly corrupted actions too. This is unchecked. pi0_check's race
  check can measure it, and it may explain part of the grasp misses.
- Agent context: the diagnosis agent's session ended during the retrain. The coordinator
  stopped the process when the user asked and wrote these notes.
