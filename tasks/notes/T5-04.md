# T5-04 notes

- **Success rate**: 100/100 in the suite test (100 seeded `fetch_scene` scenes, one target per
  scene cycling through all five block indices, pairs and single). Off-suite, every block of
  the same 100 scenes as target: 497/500 (99.4%); the 3 misses are at the far edge (r > 0.32 m).
- **Physics**: timestep 2 ms (so101.xml's 5 ms let blocks jitter), implicitfast, elliptic cones,
  impratio 10, noslip_iterations 3. Blocks 15 g, condim 4, friction (1.2, 0.01, 2e-4); jaws
  friction 1.5; table 0.6; solref 0.004 on jaws and blocks. Actuator gains untouched (sts3215
  kp 998, forcerange 2.94); jaw commanded to -0.17 so it squeezes at the force limit.
- **What needed tuning**: mostly kinematics, not contact. With the jaws vertical the arm reaches
  only ~0.32 m at table height (wrist_flex hits its limit), but the workspace corner is 0.36 m,
  and higher up the wrist runs out sooner. So the planner leans the gripper outwards by the
  least tilt (0-36 deg, 6 deg steps) that reaches, per pose, and plans a pose every 1 cm on the
  vertical line (runtime IK with clipping stalled at the limit). Among the 4 face-aligned yaws
  it prefers a collision-free path, then least tilt, then (tilted) the fixed jaw on the outer
  side: the fixed jaw's collision hull sits low and otherwise lands on the block.
- API: `PhysWorld.reset/step/arm_qpos/ctrl/block_position/block_yaw/lifted/render_camera`;
  `expert.pick(world, i, on_tick=None) -> PickResult(success, steps, ctrl_log, reason)`, one
  ctrl row per 20 ms tick; `on_tick(world, ctrl)` is the hook for T5-05 recording.
- **Worries**: (1) occluded scenes (`occlude=True`) pick the hidden block only ~18/40: the box
  is 2 cm from the block and the gripper or arm clips it; also fetch_scene's box can overlap
  other blocks, which physics then shoves aside. Matters if T5-05 records "hidden" demos.
  (2) Scene-building is duplicated from `world.build_model` (it compiles, so the spec can't be
  extended); a shared `build_spec()` in world.py would remove that. (3) No wrist-cam use yet.
