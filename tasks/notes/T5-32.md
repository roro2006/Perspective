- Credit: navigation + walking policies by kouylty (huggingface.co/kouylty/Isaac-Go2-Navigation-Rough-v0
  @6c61ab2, MIT), trained in NVIDIA Isaac Lab (BSD-3); Go2 model by Unitree Robotics (Menagerie,
  BSD-3). 10.7 MB in third_party/, nothing else downloaded. README on the hub is empty.
- export_go2_nav.py: both TorchScript actors equal their training checkpoints; numpy vs torch max
  diff 7.6e-6 (walk) and 1.9e-6 (nav); 64 samples each stored for the CPU test.
- Walking policy: Isaac Lab Go2 rough 235-d layout, so T5-20's port reused unchanged. Nav (203-d,
  empirical normaliser): lin vel, ang vel, gravity, pose command (x, y, z base-yaw frame, heading
  error), 17x11 scan, last raw action. Read from normaliser stats, first-layer weights, critic and
  sign probes. Flat-ground equivalents: scan as T5-20, pose z = 0.4 - base z. Nav at 5 Hz; its
  action clipped to [-1, 1] for the walker (raw output is 2-9). All choices in the card, written
  and committed before the run.
- Gate (run once, out/t5_32/gate.json, gate.md): **FAIL on the port.** P1 PASS (10 x 20 s, 0 falls,
  min base z 0.26). P2 FAIL: 0/20 within 0.3 m at 20 s [0.00, 0.16], 13 falls. Curio lines not
  run (as the gate says). T5-20 path follower on the dog scene, for comparison: 10/10 [0.72, 1.00],
  0 falls.
- Diagnosis: the policy does reach every goal (closest approach <= 0.3 m on 18/20) but cannot
  stop. Its raw actions exceed 1 on 97-99% of steps (median max 2.5-3), so the command is
  bang-bang; at the goal it flips between the [-1, 1] corners every 0.2 s and the walker falls.
  Its training statistics agree it never stood still (mean vx 0.45 m/s, goal mostly within 0.3 m).
- Open choices: the pose-command z (training mean 0.37 vs the flat-ground 0.07) and the clip are
  my reading; porting checks with z = 0.37, unclipped, or zeroed last action were no better.
- Worry: the dog scene's goal is ~9 m away around a corner; even a working policy needs the map
  route (eval hands it the scene's door waypoints), and its 1.6 x 1.0 m scan cannot see walls.
